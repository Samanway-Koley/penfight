The official(ish) rulebook

How to win a pen fight.

Two pens. One desk. The last pen still on the wood takes the round. It is simple — right up until someone aims for the edge.

  1. 01

    Pull back from your pen

    Press on your own pen and drag away from your target, then release. Force builds gradually: a short pull is a gentle tap, and only a long pull into the red is a full-strength shot.

  2. 02

    Work it toward the edge

    Hit off-centre to add spin. Ball pens slide far but hit lightly, gel grips stay predictable, and fountain pens hit hardest. The next turn unlocks only when both pens stop.

  3. 03

    Stay on the desk

    A pen falls when its centre crosses the desk edge. Flicking yourself off without touching your rival is a foul. If both pens fall, the one that fell later loses; a dead tie is a draw and the round is replayed.

Desk rule: first to 2 rounds wins a best-of-3, first to 3 wins a best-of-5. The opening break alternates every round.
